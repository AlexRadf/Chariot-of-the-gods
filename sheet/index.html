Dice & Pushing

Dice rolls

  • Base dice = Attribute + Skill + Gear.
  • Stress dice = +1 die per Stress Level. A 1 on a stress die triggers a Panic Roll.
  • Modifiers adjust the number of dice — remove from base dice first, then stress.
  • Results: every 6 is a success. Extra 6s buy Stunts.

Pushing

If no 1s show on your stress dice, you may push: add 1 Stress Level (and its new stress die), then reroll all non-6s. You can only push once (some talents allow twice).

Story Points

  • Gain 1 by playing your agenda (max 3).
  • Spend 1 to add a 6 to a roll.

Stress & Panic

Panic Roll — d6 + current Stress Level

≤6Keeping it together. You hold your nerve. Barely.
7Nervous twitch. Your Stress Level and that of all friendly PCs in Short range increase by one.
8Tremble. All AGILITY rolls suffer −2 until your panic stops.
9Drop item. You drop a weapon/important item (GM's choice). Stress +1.
10Freeze. Frozen for one Round, losing your next action. You and friendly PCs in Short range gain 1 stress.
11Seek cover. Next action must move to safety (retreat roll allowed if enemy Engaged). Your stress −1; friendly PCs in Short range +1.
12Scream. Lose next action. Your stress −1, but every friendly who hears must make a Panic Roll.
13Flee. Flee to safety and refuse to leave. Your stress −1; everyone who sees you run must Panic Roll.
14Psychotic. Attack the nearest person or creature until you or the target is Broken. Witnesses must Panic Roll.
15+Catatonic. You collapse, unable to move or speak.

Duration: 7/9/10/11/12 immediate; 8/13/14/15+ last until a Stop Panic action, Broken, or 1 Turn passes.

Gain Stress when…

  • You push a skill roll.
  • You fire full-auto.
  • You suffer one or more points of damage.
  • You go without sleep, food, or water.
  • You perform a coup de grâce.
  • A Scientist fails to Analyze something.
  • A crewmate attacks you.
  • Someone nearby is revealed to be an android.
  • You meet certain creatures/places (per scenario/GM).

Make a Panic Roll if…

  • You roll one or more 1s on your stress dice (you can't push — roll Panic instead).
  • You witness a friendly suffering certain panic effects.
  • You're pinned down by a ranged attack.
  • You suffer a critical injury.
  • You're attacked by a strange creature you've never seen.
  • A truly terrifying event occurs (per scenario/GM).

Synthetics

General rules

  • Attributes: +3 to two attributes of choice. Skills: can't push rolls.
  • Stress & Panic: none — no Stress Level, never Panic.
  • Don't need air, food, water or sleep; immune to vacuum, cold and disease; typically unaffected by alien pathogens.
  • Don't heal — a Shift of work + COMTECH roll repairs all lost Health and crits (unless in System Shutdown). Can self-repair.
  • Never make Death Rolls. On System Shutdown they can be reactivated (power + Turn + COMTECH), but gain permanent mental trauma.

Critical injuries (d6)

1Ruptured fluid pump — lose next slow action.
2Destroyed leg servos — must stay prone, can only crawl.
3Destroyed arm servos — one-handed gear only.
4Head dislocation — all fast actions become slow.
5Severe chassis breach — completely immobilized.
6System shutdown — needs a COMTECH roll to communicate.

A Broken android keeps operating with its crit until System Shutdown. Repeat crits shift one step down the table.

Xenomorphs

General rules

  • No attributes — use listed skills. Never push rolls. Have natural armor.
  • Higher Speed (2+): move 2 zones per Speed in stealth; draw an initiative card per Speed in combat (slow + fast on each).
  • Signature attacks: roll a d6 on the creature's table at Engaged; blockable only with a weapon/blunt instrument.
  • At 0 Health they're not Broken — roll a d6 crit below.

Critical injuries (d6)

1Rise again — plays dead; regains 1 Health and rises if attacked or on its next turn.
2Wounded — Speed −1, regains 1 Health; may try to escape (1–3 each Round).
3Desperate action — free fast + slow action; escapes or attacks nearest.
4Last breath — kills nearest on its next turn, then dies (dies instantly if wounded first).
5–6Torn apart — instant death; Analyze only at OBSERVATION −2.
