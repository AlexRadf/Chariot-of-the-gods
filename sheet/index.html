    Quick Rules

    Rolling & pushing

    How to roll

    • Your pool = Attribute + Skill (+ any gear or modifier), plus stress dice equal to your Stress Level.
    • Every 6 is a success. One success means you make it; extra 6s buy stunts. No 6s means you fail.

    Pushing

    • Failed, or want more? Push once — reroll every die that isn't a 6.
    • Pushing raises your Stress Level by 1 and adds a stress die.
    • If a stress die shows a 1 on a push, you can't push again — make a Panic Roll.

    Stress & panic

    Panic Roll — d6 + Stress Level

    ≤6Keeping it together — no effect.
    7Nervous twitch — you and friendlies in Short range gain 1 stress.
    8Tremble — all AGILITY rolls −2 until panic stops.
    9Drop item — you drop something; stress +1.
    10Freeze — lose your next action; you and friendlies near gain 1 stress.
    11Seek cover — must move to safety before anything else.
    12Scream — lose your action; everyone who hears must Panic Roll.
    13Flee — run to safety and refuse to leave.
    14Berserk — attack the nearest character until you or they are Broken.
    15+Catatonic — you collapse, unable to move or speak.

    You gain stress when…

    • you push a roll; you take damage; you fire full-auto;
    • you go without sleep, food or water;
    • a crewmate attacks you, or a horrifying truth comes to light;
    • you face something truly terrifying.

    Stress only comes down with rest, a safe moment, or when the GM says the pressure lifts.

    Actions in a round

    Slow actions

    Close attackCLOSE COMBAT, usually Engaged. 6s: +1 damage, grapple, disarm, knock prone.
    Shoot / throwRANGED COMBAT + range mods. 6s: +1 damage, pin down, disarm, knock back.
    Full autoShoot at +2 and +1 stress; spread extra 6s to nearby targets.
    First aidMEDICAL AID to get a Broken ally up; each 6 heals 1 Health.
    Give ordersCOMMAND an ally who can hear you; each 6 gives +1 to their roll.
    ManipulateMANIPULATION to get someone to comply (or they must attack you).
    Stop panicCOMMAND to bring a panicking ally back to their senses.

    Fast actions

    Aim+2 on a ranged attack made right after.
    BlockReaction vs an armed/creature attack. 6s: −1 damage, counter, disarm.
    Draw / get upReady an item, or stand (attacks vs you get +2 until you do).
    Shove / retreatPush an enemy to Short, or MOBILITY to disengage to Short.
    RunMove to an adjacent zone (or Short→Engaged in the same zone).
    Seek coverUse nearby cover's Armor Rating vs ranged attacks.
    Help+1 to an ally's action per helper (max +3).

    Stealth & sneak attacks
    • In stealth you move up to 2 zones per turn. You automatically spot passive enemies in your zone or line of sight.
    • Sneak past or ambush: roll MOBILITY vs their OBSERVATION — success means a free attack.
